File:Ming Xiaoling Mausoleum (54438090344).jpg — xiquinhosilva — CC BY 2.0 — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ming_Xiaoling_Mausoleum_(54438090344).jpg — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0
图像说明：The Ming Xiaoling Mausoleum, resting at the southern base of Purple Mountain in Nanjing, Jiangsu, China, is a site of rich history and architectural grandeur. Constructed in the 14th century, it serves as the final resting place of the Hongwu Emperor, founder of the Ming dynasty. 

This mausoleum is renowned for its Sacred Way, a path lined with stone animals and statues leading to the tomb, symbolizing the emperor's journey to the afterlife. The intricate design and historical significance of the Ming Xiaoling Mausoleum have earned it a place as a UNESCO World Heritage Site, marking it as a treasure of cultural heritage.
